Where to start

PickOneProcess.ShipIt.ThenExtend.

The fastest way to doubt automation is to automate everything at once. We prefer one workflow, measured properly, then a second that inherits the same architecture.
01

Start with volume

The workflow people complain about most is usually the one that repeats most — that is where hours come back fastest.

02

Keep a human in the loop

Anything with money, promises or escalation potential stays reviewed. Automation prepares, people decide.

03

Measure before and after

Time per case, response time, missed follow-ups. Three numbers are enough to know whether it worked.

04

Then reuse the pattern

Once intake, routing and notification exist, the next workflow is a fraction of the effort.
